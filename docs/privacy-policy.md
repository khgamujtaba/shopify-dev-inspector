# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

## Introduction

Shopify Dev Inspector respects your privacy. This extension is designed to inspect publicly available Shopify storefront information and does not collect, store, or share personal information.

## Information We Collect

Shopify Dev Inspector does not collect any personal information.

The extension does not collect:

- Personal data
- Browsing history
- Login credentials
- Payment information
- Cookies
- Analytics data

## How the Extension Works

The extension reads publicly available information from the currently active webpage to identify Shopify storefronts and display store-related information such as:

- Shopify platform detection
- Theme name (when publicly available)
- Theme version (when publicly available)
- Store language
- Store currency
- Shopify Hydrogen/Oxygen detection

All processing is performed locally within your browser.

No information is transmitted to external servers.

## Data Sharing

Shopify Dev Inspector does not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties.

## Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

### activeTab

Used to inspect the currently active webpage after the user opens the extension.

### scripting

Used to read publicly available Shopify information from the active webpage.

### host_permissions

Required to inspect Shopify storefront information on websites that the user chooses to visit.

## Third-Party Services

Shopify Dev Inspector does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
